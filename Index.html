That Was Me Once

I was in my third year at a public university, with no savings and no idea where my money was going. Somehow, I always found myself broke before the month ended, and sometimes even before it was halfway through.

I started cooking in bulk, jollof mostly, and portioning it into plastic bowls just to make it last until my next allowance came in.

Then one month, my parents' transfer was delayed. I'd already run out of food, and my jollof stash was finished too, so I had to borrow money from friends just to eat.

❗ That was my wake-up call.

I decided to learn how to budget properly, find a method that actually worked for me, and build the discipline to stick to it. I even made a makeshift budget binder from a notebook and sticky notes, and used it until I finally got a proper one.

Over time, I stopped living from allowance to allowance and broke the cycle of always being broke.

Whether you are preparing for university or already on campus, this guide will help you take control of your money, build better habits, and break that same cycle.
